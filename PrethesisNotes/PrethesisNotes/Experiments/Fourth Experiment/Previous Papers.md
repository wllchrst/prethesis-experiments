### A Comparison of BiLSTM, BERT, and Ensemble Method for Emotion Recognition on Indonesian Product Reviews
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1877050924030746

- Slang Translation
- Stemming
Data Augmentation are also implemented on the dataset, but at the end of the day our results still better than the paper.
